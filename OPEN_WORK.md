| HTTPS for the app origin                   | A service worker only registers in a secure context, and `http://<host>:4200` is not one — so offline on the phone is blocked until the app is served over TLS. Put Caddy in front of both (static build + `/api/*` proxied to FastAPI, same origin) and reach it over Tailscale: its `<machine>.<tailnet>.ts.net` name carries a publicly trusted certificate, so nothing is installed on the phone — a local `mkcert` CA would be a universal trust anchor whose key sits unencrypted on the laptop. Decide the origin **before** M5: the service worker cache and IndexedDB are origin-bound, so changing it later discards everything cached.
